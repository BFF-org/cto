## Results for the Crack the Orange scholarship program by Bitcoin for Fairness

The online learning platform launched in 2023, and in 2024 we opened the course program, pairing it with live calls and a community program for aspiring Bitcoin educators, developers, translators, and community builders across the Global South. Students could apply for a scholarship and join the program for 12 months.

We reviewed 444 applications in detail, awarded 341 scholarships, and saw 156 graduates from 20 African countries complete the program successfully. The program had its strongest impact in Zambia, with 41 graduates, followed by Malawi with 18, Mozambique with 17, and Zimbabwe with 14. These results reflect the groundwork Bitcoin for Fairness has laid on the ground since 2020, supporting local initiatives starting in Zimbabwe and Zambia.
